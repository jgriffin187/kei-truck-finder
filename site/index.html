    HA7 or TT2 "confirmed" means the chassis number shows HA7 (Acty) or TT2 (Sambar). "Likely" means a 2000 or later Acty truck listed as 4WD, which is always an HA7, or a 4WD Sambar truck, which is always a TT2. Sambars are limited to 2000 and newer trucks with a manual gearbox. When a listing gives only a year, the build month is assumed to be December, so the legal date shown is the latest it could be. Ask the seller for the exact build month before you pay. Delivered prices are to Long Beach/Los Angeles. Exchange rate: yen per dollar.
